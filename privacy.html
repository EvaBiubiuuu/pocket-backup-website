Privacy policy

Your details, handled properly

Last updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Pocket Backup collects through this website, why, and what you can do about it. Pocket Backup is a small Queensland business. I aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988.

What I collect

  • Contact form: your name, business name, mobile, email, network, job volume and anything you write in the message.
  • Booking a call: the details you enter on Calendly, such as your name, email and the time you choose.
  • Emails and messages you send me.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not track you across other sites.

Why I collect it

Only to reply to you, book and hold your call, and give you a quote. I don't sell, rent or trade your details, and I don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Where it's stored

I use a few trusted services to run this website. They may store information on servers outside Australia, including in the United States:

  • Netlify hosts this website and receives contact form submissions. Like most web hosts, it keeps basic technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Calendly handles call bookings.
  • Google (Gmail) handles email.

If you become a client

Information about your business, jobs and customers that you share for the work itself is kept confidential and used only for your work. I use my own login in your systems, never your bank details, and hand everything back when we stop working together.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't go ahead are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept as long as the law requires for business and tax records.

Seeing, correcting or deleting your details

Email qld.pocketbackup@gmail.com and I'll reply within 5 business days. If you're not happy with how I handle a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
