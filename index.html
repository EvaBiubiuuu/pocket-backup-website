    Questions

    Common questions

    How is this different from hiring a VA?

    A VA sells you hours and waits for instructions. We sell you finished jobs at a fixed price, we set ourselves up, and we already know Energex, Ergon and STCs. You don't manage us, you get a report.

    Do I have to give you my passwords?

    No. You add us as our own user in your systems (Energex/Ergon portal, STC agent, ServiceM8 or Tradify, Xero). You can see what we do and remove us any time. We never change bank details.

    Who is responsible if something gets rejected?

    You stay responsible for the installation and the accuracy of what you send us, as the licence holder. We're responsible for checking it, lodging it correctly and on time. If a rejection is our error, we fix it free and that job is free.

    Do customer payments go through you?

    Never. Invoices go out from your own system and every payment, including STC money, goes straight to your account. We only invoice you for our service.

    What if I only do a few solar jobs a month?

    Pay per job. There's no minimum and no contract. Monthly plans only make sense once you're doing around five or more jobs a month.

    Which STC agent and job software do you work with?

    The ones you already use. We work in your accounts, so nothing changes for your customers or your suppliers.

    What happens if we stop?

    Give 14 days' notice on a monthly plan, nothing on per-job. We finish or hand over open jobs, give you the playbook we wrote, and remove our access.
